Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy applies to the "Wait, Really?" social media accounts and the private tool ("FactSnips Studio") used to create and publish their videos.

What this tool does

FactSnips Studio is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its owner to generate and upload short fact videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

Data accessed

The tool uses official platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API) solely to upload content to the owner's own accounts. It accesses only the accounts it is authorized for and does not read, store or share data belonging to other users.

Data collected

The tool does not collect personal data from viewers or any third parties. It stores authentication tokens locally on the owner's device only to sign in to the owner's own accounts.

Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.
